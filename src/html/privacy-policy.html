Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

Daily Mind Challenge ("we", "us", or "the site") offers daily Wordle, Sudoku, and Word Search puzzles. This page explains what information we collect when you use the site, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information. You can play as a Guest or with a Registered account. Guest accounts only store a display name you choose — no email or password is required. Registered accounts store a username, display name, and password (handled securely by Firebase Authentication); an email address is optional and only used for password recovery.

Gameplay data. We store the puzzles you've played, your scores, points, streaks, and leaderboard standing, so your progress can be saved and displayed back to you and (where applicable) on public leaderboards.

Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are visited and how often — so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and not used to individually identify you.

Cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Consent Notice for details on what's used and why.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core features — saving your progress, scoring, and leaderboards.
  • To keep you signed in between visits.
  • To understand overall site usage and improve the experience.
  • To communicate with you if you've provided a recovery email (e.g. for password resets).

Advertising

We use, or intend to use, Google AdSense to display ads on the site. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Services

The site is built on Google Firebase (authentication, database, and hosting) and Google Analytics. We don't sell your personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by these providers as part of operating the site, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, reach out via the contact details below.

Children's Privacy

Daily Mind Challenge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent to support@dailymindchallenge.com. See our About & Contact page for more ways to reach us, or our Terms of Service for the rules that apply when you use the Site.
