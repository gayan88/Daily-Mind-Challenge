What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. Sites also use similar technologies, like local storage, to remember information between visits. We use a mix of these to run the site and understand how it's used.

Essential

Used to keep you signed in (as a Guest or a Registered user) between visits, and to remember basic preferences. Without these, core features like saving your progress and scores wouldn't work.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site — which pages get visited, how often, and general usage patterns — so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and isn't used to individually identify you. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

Advertising

We use, or intend to use, Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies to serve ads that are more relevant to you, based on your visits here and to other sites. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.

Meta Pixel

If you accept cookies, we use the Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). The pixel only loads after you click Accept; if you decline, it never runs in your browser.

When it runs, it records each page view on this site and sends technical details your browser shares, such as the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device information, and your IP address. Meta uses cookies to link these visits to a Facebook or Instagram account, if you have one, so it can show you relevant ads on those platforms and measure how they perform. The cookies Meta sets include _fbp (first-party, set by this site) and fr (set by Meta on facebook.com). You can read more in Meta's cookies policy.

You can manage Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the site — like staying signed in — from working correctly.

To change your cookie choice on this site, clear this site's stored data in your browser settings (this removes the saved Accept/Decline choice). The banner will then appear again on your next visit, and you can choose again.
